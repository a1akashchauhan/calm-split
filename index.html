How it works

From group chat to hisaab, in three quiet steps

  1. Chat like you always doEveryone drops spends in the trip group. "petrol 2770", "dinner 2400 no rohit". No rules.
  2. Export the chat, drop it hereIn WhatsApp: ⋮ → More → Export chat → Without media. Upload the file to CalmSplit.
  3. Check, save, shareAI fills the dashboard. Untick anyone who wasn't in, press Calculate and save, share one link.
